About

I'm a Computer Science student at the Colorado School of Mines, focusing on Robotic & Intelligent Systems with a minor in Electrical Engineering. Most of what I know about writing real software came from robots that had to work right now, on a field, in front of judges — there's no better teacher than a deadline that doesn't move.

As Lead Programmer for FRC Team 4738, I split my time between writing competition code and mentoring ten-plus newer programmers through the same mistakes I made a year earlier. I like systems that touch the physical world — kinematics, control loops, sensors that lie to you sometimes — as much as I like clean full-stack apps that just work.

Outside of code: I recruit for a 90-member fraternity chapter, and I used to teach sailing to kids who occasionally tried to steer straight into the dock.
